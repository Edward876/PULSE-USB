Pulse/Phone capture

Choose the lens by the flash.

Keep this page open on your Android phone. Connect via USB and open localhost using the ADB instructions in the README.

Android does not reliably report which physical lens sits nearest the flash. Check each preview and choose the rear lens directly beside it.

Camera and light permissions stay under your control.

Use the preview to select the rear lens nearest the flash. Then start the USB stream, cover that lens gently with your fingertip, and press Start measurement on the desktop. Avoid pressure and movement. Remove your finger and stop the light if it gets warm or uncomfortable.

Educational prototype, not a medical device. Video is sent to your local desktop for processing; nothing is saved. Desktop monitor
